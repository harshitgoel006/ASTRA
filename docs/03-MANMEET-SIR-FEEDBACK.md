# ASTRA --- Manmeet Sir Feedback

## Context

Manmeet Sir is an encryption/cybersecurity professor whom the human
developer met during the Encryption campus hiring process at DIT.

The human reached out to explain ASTRA and explicitly requested honest
criticism and technical challenges rather than simple encouragement.

## Message sent to Sir --- substance

The human explained:

-   ASTRA is a technical concept being documented;
-   the central question is whether AI can understand and continuously
    engineer an entire software system rather than merely generate code;
-   ASTRA goes beyond a coding assistant;
-   the concept includes system-level understanding, persistent
    engineering memory, specialized agents, verification and controlled
    autonomy;
-   the immediate career priority is securing a software engineering
    role after graduation;
-   ASTRA is intended as a serious long-term project alongside the
    career;
-   the current stage is architectural validation and identification of
    fundamental limitations;
-   honest technical criticism is specifically desired.

## Sir's response

Sir said, in substance:

-   he would like to know more about how the project is being planned;
-   from the surface, ASTRA looks like something toward which the
    industry is heading;
-   complete software development automation is not currently in place;
-   missing protocols and limited access available to AI in a sandbox
    environment are important barriers;
-   core SDLC, Agentic AI, Networking and Security are important areas
    to touch;
-   security should include both post-deployment software security and
    developmental security.

He also said:

-   keep building and share about it;
-   primarily get a good job first;
-   industry experience will provide experience and sharpen
    project-management skills.

Later, Sir advised:

-   take small milestones;
-   keep building;
-   bring milestones to public attention;
-   LinkedIn is a useful platform for exposing the work;
-   whenever a feature is achieved, post about it;
-   find people who can collaborate with the project.

He also asked how the campus drive was going.

## Human response to Sir

The human agreed to:

-   build ASTRA incrementally;
-   make milestones technically buildable, testable and demonstrable;
-   explore SDLC, Agentic AI, networking, developmental security and
    post-deployment security more deeply;
-   document progress publicly as meaningful milestones are reached;
-   find complementary collaborators as the project grows;
-   prioritize securing a software engineering role while continuing
    ASTRA as a long-term project.

## How Codex should use this feedback

This is EXTERNAL FEEDBACK.

It is not proof that ASTRA's architecture is correct.

It is a valuable expert perspective that highlights research/engineering
areas:

1.  Core SDLC
2.  Agentic AI
3.  Networking
4.  Developmental security
5.  Post-deployment security
6.  Sandbox/environment access
7.  Protocols for AI/software-system interaction
8.  Incremental, testable milestones
9.  Public milestone sharing
10. Collaboration

These areas should influence investigation and milestone design, but
technical claims must still be researched or experimentally validated.
